Research

We build robots and sensing systems that stay reliable outside the lab, where terrain, weather, lighting, and water conditions are hard to predict.

image / video

Autonomous Agricultural Vehicles

Efficient and safe motion planning lets tractors and field robots operate in tight, cluttered spaces such as orchard headlands. We also study the energy and economics of electric and hybrid tractors.

image / video

Aerial Robotics

Small UAVs for agricultural and environmental monitoring. We work on learning-based navigation, energy-aware planning, and flight control that holds up in wind and near the ground.

image / video

Aquaculture & Marine Robotics

Underwater vehicles that inspect offshore aquaculture infrastructure such as fish net cages, with robust control against currents and other disturbances.

image / video

Sensing & Safe Autonomy

Multi-sensor fusion for mapping, plus formal methods that help autonomous systems detect faults and keep operating safely.
