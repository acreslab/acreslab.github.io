ACRES Lab

Autonomy, Control, and Robotics for Environment and Sustainability

Welcome to the ACRES Lab in the Department of Bioproducts and Biosystems Engineering at the University of Minnesota, affiliated with the Precision Agriculture Center and the Minnesota Robotics Institute. We develop robust, autonomous robotic and sensing systems for sustainable and precision agriculture and environmental management. See our research and publications.
