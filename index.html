ACRES Lab

Autonomy, Control, and Robotics for Environment and Sustainability

We develop robust, autonomous, and AI-driven robotic and sensing systems for sustainable agriculture and environmental management. Leveraging both learning-based and model-based approaches, we build intelligent robots and sensing platforms that operate reliably in unstructured, real-world environments. Our current research topics include:

  • Robot Learning and Manipulation
  • Autonomous Navigation and Control
  • Multimodal Perception and 3D Reconstruction
  • Heterogeneous Robot Teaming
  • AI-Enabled Agricultural and Environmental Sensing
