ACRES Lab

Autonomy, Control, and Robotics for Environment and Sustainability

We develop robust, autonomous robotic and sensing systems for sustainable agriculture and environmental management. Our research spans robot learning and manipulation, 3D reconstruction, heterogeneous robot teams, AI-powered sensing, and autonomous navigation, enabling ground, aerial, and underwater robots to operate reliably in unstructured, real-world environments.
